## Trace defect: incomplete logs, labelled separately

The f6121342 driver called `process.exit(0)` right after the run. stdout to a pipe is asynchronous
in Node, so queued trace lines were dropped. On the pipe-volume fixture, the old driver delivered
1,782 of 254,307 lines and the fixed driver delivered all 254,307. **This is a defect in my driver,
not a gap in toyvm's timer instrumentation:** `DosLoop.raise()` both counts `irqs` and calls
`onIrq` for every interrupt.

|---|---|---|
| BLIQ | 384/384 head; 381/381 in each other tree | COMPLETE |
| CYCLE | 1205, 993, 975, 644 and 927 of 113,998 | INCOMPLETE (prefix) |

- BLIQ's PIT io trace has no counter to check it against. Its last line is at 79.87M of the 80M
  dispatches, so it is probably complete, but that is unproven.
- A dropped tail leaves an exact prefix, so a first divergence found inside it is the true first
  divergence. The line totals in result.json's `irq` and `io` entries are wrong for CYCLE and
  CAVEIRA.

First divergences (head vs stack, `hb=` ignored):

- **BLIQ** (complete): one timer IRQ, the 48th line, is delivered at 13,804,137 instead of
  13,732,896. That is 71,241 dispatches (about 7.1 ms of guest time) later. BLIQ's handler
  reprograms the PIT on every tick (43<-30, 40<-37, 40<-5d), so the PIT writes follow it to
  @13.8042M with the same values. After that, 3 fewer IRQs and 7 fewer INTs.
- **CYCLE** (prefix): the 3rd timer IRQ is at 22,515,463 instead of 22,515,339 (124 dispatches

These are timer-delivery instants moved by the schedule patches. PLAN.md item 4 asks whether each
move is class (a), (b) or (c). These lines alone do not answer that, because they do not show
whether head's instant was an early handback landing on a date. **The divergences are
UNCLASSIFIED.** CAVEIRA's large audio difference keeps the "accept the new audio" question open.
